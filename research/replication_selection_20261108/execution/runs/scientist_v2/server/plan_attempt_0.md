The script generates matched synthetic datasets for stable-only, shortcut-exposed, and late-masked training, using ten fixed seeds and the same five-epoch budget. Inputs are standardized with statistics from the training split, and validation loss and accuracy are recorded and printed at every epoch. It also evaluates shortcut-free test accuracy and loss, correlated-validation reliance under shortcut ablation, and reports mean differences with bootstrap confidence intervals. Training curves and summary comparisons are saved as plots, while metrics and relevant arrays are saved in `working/experiment_data.npy`. GPU is used when available; otherwise it runs on CPU.
