The target is an expert sensory score (median of at least three evaluations), whereas the 11 predictors are objective physicochemical measurements. The score's stated range is 0 to 10. The data card says grape types, brand and selling price are absent for privacy/logistic reasons, and notes the ordered, imbalanced score classes and possible predictor correlations. These omissions and measurement distinctions bound what the table can directly establish.
