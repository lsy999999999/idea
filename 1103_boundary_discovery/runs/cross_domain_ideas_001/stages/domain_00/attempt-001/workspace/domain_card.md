# Domain Card: Non-equilibrium statistical physics

## Portable structures

1. **Irreversibility as a ratio of path probabilities.** Compare a forward trajectory
   with its time-reversed counterpart; their probability asymmetry encodes entropy
   production and yields fluctuation relations. This reframes a question from “what is
   the average outcome?” to “which histories are suppressed or enhanced, and by what
   asymmetry?” Assumptions: a defined forward/reverse ensemble and time-reversal map;
   for the standard stochastic-thermodynamic derivation, Markov dynamics with local
   detailed balance and an explicit accounting of relevant reservoirs/variables.

2. **A constrained action over histories.** Describe a system by time-dependent state
   and flux fields, require them to obey a conservation law, and assign a cost to each
   admissible history. The most likely rare event is the minimum-cost history under the
   target constraint. This changes the question from fitting a static relation to asking
   which temporally organized path and compensating flux realize an event. Assumptions:
   a hydrodynamic or coarse-grained description with known constitutive current and
   fluctuation cost; standard MFT relies on driven diffusive scaling and specified
   transport coefficients.

3. **Dynamical phases from large-deviation singularities.** Study the scaled cumulant
   generating function or rate function of time-integrated observables; nonanalytic
   changes can indicate a change in the dominant class of histories, even when ordinary
   averages vary smoothly. This makes the ensemble of paths, rather than only a typical
   state, the object to classify. Assumptions: a suitable long-time/large-system limit
   and an observable whose large-deviation function is well-defined; interpreting a
   singularity as a phase transition requires the limit and boundary conditions to be
   specified.

## Open tensions

- How should entropy production and fluctuation relations be formulated when coarse-
  graining hides slow variables, memory, or channels that carry dissipation? Distinct
  microscopic dynamics can induce the same observed trajectories while differing in
  their time-reversal asymmetry.
- When do non-equilibrium stationary states require a nonlocal quasipotential, and how
  are that nonlocality and long-range correlations related to boundary driving,
  conservation laws, and competing optimal histories?
- Which fluctuation symmetries and path-space phase transitions survive outside the
  standard assumptions of Markovian dynamics, local detailed balance, and diffusive
  hydrodynamic scaling, especially with multiple coupled conserved quantities?
