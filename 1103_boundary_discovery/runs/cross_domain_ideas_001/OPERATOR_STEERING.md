# Idea-first execution priority

For disciplinary_input stages, two or three well-chosen accessible abstracts or source excerpts are enough to describe known structures. Do not turn input preparation into an exhaustive full-text review. Spend the remaining time articulating useful conceptual tensions. Do not evaluate the feasibility of possible cross-field projects.

The synthesis stage should produce the requested ideas, including ambitious unresolved ones, without a practical-readiness gate. The final checker alone compares similar directions and checks obvious mathematical/logical errors. Its literature scan should be focused and bounded; a failed search remains unclear. No scientific experiments, numerical benchmarks, or proof search in this run.
