Title: Institutional Ecology, 'Translations' and Boundary Objects: Amateurs and Professionals in Berkeley's Museum of Vertebrate Zoology, 1907-39
Authors: Susan Leigh Star and James R. Griesemer
Journal: Social Studies of Science 19(3), 387-420 (1989)
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1177/030631289019003001
Abstract excerpt: Scientific work is heterogeneous, requiring many different actors and viewpoints, and also requires cooperation. The case study describes how standardization of methods and boundary objects translated between viewpoints. Boundary objects are adaptable to different viewpoints while robust enough to maintain identity; the paper distinguishes repositories, ideal types, coincident boundaries, and standardized forms.
