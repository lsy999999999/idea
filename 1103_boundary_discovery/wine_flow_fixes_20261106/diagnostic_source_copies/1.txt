Source: Cortez et al. (2009), “Modeling wine preferences by data mining from physicochemical properties,” Decision Support Systems 47(4), 547–553, DOI 10.1016/j.dss.2009.05.016.
Access status: Crossref bibliographic metadata retrieved; its record has no abstract. Publisher/preprint full-text request did not complete. The statements below are the UCI dataset card's description of the cited study, not a verbatim paper abstract.

UCI describes two Portuguese Vinho Verde datasets, red and white, with objective physicochemical input tests and sensory quality output. The quality score is the median of at least three wine-expert evaluations on a 0–10 scale. It says the cited study applied several data-mining methods for regression and reports that support vector machines achieved the best results among those tried; it also reports sensitivity-analysis relative importances. These are published-source claims as relayed by UCI, not newly checked results.
